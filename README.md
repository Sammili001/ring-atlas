# Ring Atlas

*A Living Archive of Emotions, Memory, and Time*

> As trees hold time in their rings, we hold memories in our days. Leave a mark, and watch your year take shape.

[**Explore the Live Website**](https://sammili001.github.io/ring-atlas/)

## Concept

Ring Atlas is an interactive emotional diary inspired by tree rings and ripples. Each month has its own organic formation, with individual rings representing the days of that month.

As users record their feelings and thoughts, each marked day's gray contour is replaced by blue ink, gradually shaping a personal visual archive. Rather than measuring emotions through numbers, Ring Atlas invites users to reflect on how everyday experiences leave different marks over time.

## Interactions

- **Leave a mark:** Select a date, choose or enter a feeling, and optionally record a thought.
- **Shape your rings:** Different feelings influence the thickness, texture, and direction of the contours.
- **Revisit memories:** Select a recorded date or ring to read and edit previous entries.
- **Explore your year:** Switch between Month View and Year Overview across 2026 and 2027.
- **Replay growth:** Watch the monthly formation grow from the center outward.

## Technical Implementation

Built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and the Canvas API, without external dependencies.

Diary entries are stored locally in each user's browser and can be exported as JSON.

Developed through an iterative design and vibe coding process with Codex, and deployed through GitHub Pages.
